# Current ML Trends in Agriculture

## 1. Precision Agriculture

ML is being used to understand field-level differences and optimize agricultural inputs.

## 2. Remote Sensing

Satellite imagery can be combined with ML to monitor crop health, vegetation and water stress.

## 3. Deep Learning

CNN models are useful for image-based crop disease detection.

LSTM and other sequence models can be used for agricultural time-series forecasting.

## 4. Explainable AI

Explainability is important because farmers and agronomists need to understand why a recommendation was generated.

## 5. Edge and Mobile AI

Mobile and edge-based systems can support agricultural decisions where internet connectivity is limited.

## 6. MLOps

Continuous monitoring and retraining are important because agricultural conditions change across seasons and locations.
