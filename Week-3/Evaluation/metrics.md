# Evaluation Metrics

## RMSE

Root Mean Squared Error measures the average magnitude of prediction errors while giving greater importance to large errors.

Lower RMSE is better.

## MAE

Mean Absolute Error measures the average absolute difference between predicted and actual yield.

Lower MAE is better.

## R²

R² measures how much variation in crop yield is explained by the model relative to a mean baseline.

Higher R² is better.

## MAPE

Mean Absolute Percentage Error can provide an intuitive percentage-based interpretation but should be used carefully when actual yield values are close to zero.

## Primary Metrics

The project will primarily use:

1. RMSE
2. MAE
3. R²

All three should be reported together rather than relying on one metric.
