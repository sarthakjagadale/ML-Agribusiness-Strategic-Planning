# SWOT Analysis – ML Crop Yield Prediction

## Strengths

* Captures nonlinear agricultural relationships.
* Can combine weather, soil, crop and vegetation information.
* Supports production and resource planning.
* Can be evaluated using multiple quantitative metrics.

## Weaknesses

* Depends heavily on data quality.
* Can be less interpretable than simple regression.
* May perform poorly in regions not represented in training data.
* Requires periodic monitoring and retraining.

## Opportunities

* Integration with satellite imagery.
* Integration with real-time weather data.
* Expansion to multiple crops.
* Integration with agricultural dashboards.
* Uncertainty-aware predictions.
* Automated model retraining.

## Threats

* Climate change can change historical relationships.
* Missing or biased agricultural data.
* Poor adoption by end users.
* Privacy and governance concerns.
* Overconfidence in incorrect predictions.

## Conclusion

The approach has strong potential for agribusiness decision support, but successful implementation requires reliable data, appropriate validation, explainability and continuous monitoring.
