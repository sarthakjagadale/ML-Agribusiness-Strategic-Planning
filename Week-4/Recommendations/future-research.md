# Future Research Directions

Future research can extend the project beyond traditional tabular machine learning.

## Multi-Modal Machine Learning

Combine:

* Weather data
* Soil data
* Crop statistics
* Satellite imagery
* Time-series information

## Deep Learning

CNN models can be investigated for satellite or agricultural images, while LSTM or other temporal architectures can be explored for sequential weather and crop-condition data.

## Uncertainty-Aware Forecasting

Future research should investigate probabilistic prediction so that agricultural stakeholders understand the confidence associated with each forecast.

## Climate-Aware Modelling

Models can be tested under changing climate conditions and extreme-weather scenarios.

## Geographic Transfer

Research can investigate whether a model trained in one region can generalize to another region.

## Responsible AI

Future work should examine:

* Fairness
* Data governance
* Privacy
* Explainability
* Human oversight

## Hybrid Models

Combining machine learning with agricultural domain knowledge or process-based crop models could provide another research direction.
